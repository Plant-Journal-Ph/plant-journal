Welcome!

This is our group's cultivation journal for the Philippine Native Plant Conservation Initiative. We grew two legumes and kept a record of how they did.

Each plant page covers its growing conditions, a 12-week observation log (light, temperature, watering, soil pH, growth stage, and problems we fixed), its expected growth timeline, pests to watch for, and what the plant is used for.

    A note on origins. Both plants grow throughout the Philippines, but neither started here. Butterfly pea is a tropical Asian vine recorded as introduced to the country, and ipil-ipil came from Mexico and Central America and is now naturalized and invasive. Each plant page gives the details.

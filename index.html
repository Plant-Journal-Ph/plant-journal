This is our little plant journal for the Philippine Native Plant Conservation Initiative. For this project our group took care of two legumes, blue ternate (butterfly pea) and ipil-ipil, and wrote down how they were doing week by week.

    wait, are they native?

    Not quite! Both grow all over the Philippines, but neither started here. Blue ternate is a tropical Asian vine that was brought in, and ipil-ipil came from Mexico and Central America. It spread so well that it's now considered invasive. More on each plant's page.
